PocketPilot
case study.

PocketPilot is a personal finance app built around the payment itself. It helps users track expenses, initiate UPI payments through the apps they already use, and organise the resulting spending by source and category so they can understand where their money is going.

PAY

UPI-first payment flow

TRACK

Expenses across UPI, cash and other spending

UNDERSTAND

Dynamic category and spending insights

The idea

Instead of making users remember to enter every expense later, PocketPilot brings payment and expense tracking closer together. The aim is to make financial tracking part of the normal payment experience.

What it does

The dashboard gives a monthly overview, while transactions can be grouped into useful categories such as food, travel, shopping and bills. UPI payments can be initiated from the app and connected to the expense context.

Product direction

The focus is practical personal finance: less manual bookkeeping, clearer spending patterns and a faster way to see where money is being used.
