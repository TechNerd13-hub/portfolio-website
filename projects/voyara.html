Voyara
case study.

Voyara is a modern travel discovery product designed around curated journeys. Instead of presenting a flat list of destinations, it combines recommendation scoring, multi-currency filtering and motion-driven discovery to make planning feel more intentional.

DISCOVER

Curated travel experiences

SCORE

Recommendation-based ranking

FILTER

USD / INR multi-currency support

The product

Voyara is designed to help a traveler move from browsing to a more confident shortlist of places and journeys.

Technology

React + Vite form the core, with React Router for navigation, Lucide icons and Framer Motion for transitions and interaction.

Design approach

Motion is used as feedback rather than decoration: content enters in deliberate sequences and interactions reinforce where the user is in the experience.
